Kebab in Como, in Monte Olimpino

Besides wood-fired pizza we make kebab and piadina. They are filled when you order, not before, and you order the kebab on the website just like the pizzas.

What we have and what it costs

  • Regular kebab
    In bread or in a piadina flatbread, filled to order. You choose the sauces at the counter.
    €5.00
  • Special kebab
    Coming soon.
  • Piadina
    Filled to order. Ask at the counter about the fillings.

Prices in euros, VAT included. The price list on display in the shop is the valid one.

How to order

You order the kebab on the website: in the menu open the "Kebab and piadina" tab, tap the kebab, choose bread or piadina and add it to your order, together with pizzas if you like.

You can collect it at the pizzeria, at Via Bellinzona 187 in Monte Olimpino, or have it delivered. Delivery costs €0.50 extra per kebab and starts from two items, pizzas or kebabs: all the details are on the pizza delivery in Como page.

Allergies and intolerances

The allergen register is available in the shop, as required by EU Reg. 1169/2011. If you have an allergy, tell the staff or call before ordering. Some products may be frozen at source.
