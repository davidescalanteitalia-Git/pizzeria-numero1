Pizza delivery in Como

We bring wood-fired pizzas to your door, Monday to Friday, up to 4 km from our pizzeria in Monte Olimpino.

What delivery costs

  • Any of the thirty pizzas on the menu€5.99
  • Kebab, in bread or piadina€5.00
  • Delivery, per pizza or kebab+€0.50
  • Minimum order for delivery2 items
  • Drink of your choice+€1.00

For example: two pizzas delivered cost €12.98. The minimum of two can be any mix of pizzas and kebabs.

When we deliver

Monday to Friday, from 6 pm to 11 pm. We do not deliver on Saturday and Sunday: the pizzeria is open as usual and you collect at the counter.

Where we deliver

We deliver up to 4 km from the pizzeria at Via Bellinzona 187, staying on the Italian side of the border. We do not deliver to Switzerland.

The closest neighbourhoods are Monte Olimpino, Ponte Chiasso, Sagnino and Tavernola. For other parts of Como, write your address in the order: we will tell you straight away whether it is within 4 km.

How to order

  • Build your order from the menu on the website: choose pizzas and kebabs, remove or add ingredients, enter your address and send it. It is the quickest way.
  • If you prefer, write on WhatsApp.
  • Or call +39 031 2073720 or +39 333 4430164.

If you would rather collect

Pickup is available every day, Saturday and Sunday included, at no extra cost. We also make kebab and piadina.
