Frequently asked questions

The things people ask us most often, in order.

How much is a pizza?

Any of the thirty pizzas on the menu costs €5.99. Add a drink of your choice for €1 more.

What are your opening hours?

We are open every day from 6 pm to 11 pm, Saturday and Sunday included.

Do you deliver?

Yes, Monday to Friday, up to 4 km from the pizzeria. Delivery costs €0.50 extra per pizza or kebab and starts from two items in total. On Saturday and Sunday orders are pickup only.

Do you deliver to Switzerland?

No. We deliver up to 4 km from the pizzeria and stay on the Italian side of the border.

How do I order?

From the menu on the website: choose pizzas and kebabs, customise them and send the order. You can also write on WhatsApp at +39 333 4430164 or call +39 031 2073720.

How can I pay?

Cash, credit or debit card, or contactless with your phone.

Is the oven really wood-fired?

Yes. The pizza comes out of a wood-fired oven, not an electric one, and we stretch the dough when your order comes in.

Can I remove or add ingredients?

Yes. Removing an ingredient is free. Each added ingredient costs €1; cured meats, tuna or buffalo mozzarella cost €2.

Do you make kebab too?

Yes. The regular kebab costs €5.00, in bread or in a piadina flatbread, filled to order. You order it on the website like the pizzas, for pickup or delivery. We also make piadina.

Can I eat in?

We are a takeaway pizzeria: you collect your pizzas at the counter or get them delivered.

I have an allergy, what should I do?

The allergen register is available in the shop, as required by EU Reg. 1169/2011. Call us before ordering and tell the staff.

Where are you?

At Via Bellinzona 187, Monte Olimpino, 22100 Como. You may also find us as Al Numero 1: same place, same phone number.
